🌿 Eco-Friendly Tourism Tips

Travel responsibly and help preserve Maharashtra's natural and cultural heritage.

♻️ Reduce Plastic

Carry reusable water bottles and avoid single-use plastic during your journey.

🌳 Protect Nature

Do not damage plants, forests, or natural habitats while visiting tourist locations.

🐅 Respect Wildlife

Maintain a safe distance from animals and never feed wildlife.

🏘️ Support Local Communities

Purchase local products and support local guides, artisans, and businesses.

🚆 Use Sustainable Transport

Prefer public transport, walking, or shared transit whenever possible.

🏛️ Protect Heritage

Respect historical monuments and never deface or damage heritage structures.
